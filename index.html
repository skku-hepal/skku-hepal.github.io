About the workshop

This workshop is a meeting point for researchers in Korea and Japan working on high-energy astrophysical phenomena: X-ray and gamma-ray observations, cosmic rays, neutrinos, compact objects, supernova remnants, active galactic nuclei, and the theory and instrumentation that connect them.

The goal is simple: get people in the same room, hear what everyone is working on, and leave with concrete joint projects. The format emphasizes short talks, generous discussion time, and dedicated collaboration sessions.

X-ray & gamma-ray astronomy

Observations with XRISM, NuSTAR, Fermi, CTA, and future missions.

Cosmic rays & neutrinos

Acceleration, propagation, and multi-messenger connections.

Compact objects

Neutron stars, black holes, pulsars, magnetars, and transients.

Instrumentation & missions

Detector development and joint mission opportunities.

Getting there

  • From Incheon Airport (ICN): Airport limousine bus to Suwon (about 1h 30m), or AREX + Seoul Metro Line 1 to Sungkyunkwan Univ. Station.
  • From Seoul: Seoul Metro Line 1 to Sungkyunkwan Univ. Station, then a 10-minute walk or shuttle bus to campus.
  • Accommodation: A list of nearby hotels will be sent to registered participants.

Participation

Participation is by invitation and free of charge. If you would like to attend or give a presentation, please contact the organizer. You will receive a registration link by email, where we ask for your name, institute, and an optional presentation title.
